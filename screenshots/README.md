# Screenshots: the guest journey, before and after

Phone screenshots at 390 × 844. "Before" is the current website, rendered from saved copies of its Wine List and Culinary Kids pages (25–26 Oct 2026). "After" is the new table menu.

## Boards (before and after, by task)

| Task | Current website | New menu | Board |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Find a red wine by the bottle | About 15 screens of scrolling, inside a box that scrolls separately from the page | 2 taps | [1-find-a-red-wine.png](boards/1-find-a-red-wine.png) |
| Switch from drinks to food | Scroll to the top, tap, wait for a new page | 1 tap | [2-switch-drinks-to-food.png](boards/2-switch-drinks-to-food.png) |
| Find the children's menu | A separate page, reached from the Menus page only | 1 tap from the start page | [3-find-the-kids-menu.png](boards/3-find-the-kids-menu.png) |
| Read comfortably and check allergens | Thin 13–16px text, grey at 3.6:1 contrast | 17px text, *Larger text* button, one-tap allergen key | [4-read-and-check-allergens.png](boards/4-read-and-check-allergens.png) |

All four on one page: [storyboard.png](boards/storyboard.png).

How the "about 15 screens" was measured: in the saved Wine List page, the first bottle reds ("Red — Soft & Juicy") start 10,535px into the wine list. The site shows its 320px-wide mobile layout scaled up 1.22× on a 390px phone, so that's about 12,800 screen pixels, or 15 screens of 844px.
